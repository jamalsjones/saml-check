saml check

Responses · Requests · Metadata

Paste a SAML response, assertion, AuthnRequest or metadata and see why a sign-in failed or what an identity provider is getting wrong. It's checked in this page; nothing is uploaded.

SAML message or metadata

Get a response from your browser's developer tools (the SAMLResponse field posted to your app) or a SAML tracer extension. Signatures aren't verified and encrypted assertions aren't decrypted; that needs keys and is your service provider's job.

Findings

Paste something, or try a sample.

In a pipeline

The same file is a command-line tool. Run it on a schedule against your identity provider's metadata URL to catch an expiring signing certificate before it takes SSO down. It exits 1 on errors.

node saml-check.js https://login.example.com/saml/metadata --cert-days 45
